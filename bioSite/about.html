Meet Stephanie

Empathetic. Creative. Inquisitive.

Her story

Stephanie M. Friedman is an attorney, traveler, marathon finisher, and someone who believes that difficult goals are worth pursuing. Growing up, Stephanie lived in Arkansas, Missouri, Texas, and Mexico. Moving between different places introduced her to a variety of environments and cultures. Her early life also brought challenges, including poverty, which she worked to overcome.

Stephanie's desire to help people who could not always help themselves inspired her to pursue a legal career. Passing the bar examination was an important milestone. She later served as the elected City Attorney of Jacksonville, Arkansas, winning election to the position twice. Her professional journey reflects a commitment to public service and the responsibility of advocating for others.

Her determination is not limited to the courtroom. Stephanie completed a marathon in Utah, a challenge that confirmed something important to her: she has the willpower to finish hard things. The experience became one of the accomplishments she values most.

Outside of work, Stephanie loves traveling and experiencing other cultures. Rome is a favorite because of its food and architecture. Santorini, Greece, stands out for its views and distinctive buildings. She also loves the Bahamas, especially its beautiful water. Her curiosity extends to language as well; she speaks Spanish.

Stephanie describes herself as empathetic, creative, and inquisitive. She wants people to know her as more than an attorney. She hopes to come across as approachable, interesting, and someone they would enjoy sharing a conversation with.

Looking ahead, Stephanie hopes to relocate, live near the beach, and begin a new professional chapter. Her story connects the challenges she has faced, the goals she has achieved, and her excitement for what comes next. For Stephanie, success is not only about a title or accomplishment. It is also about remaining curious, embracing new experiences, and making meaningful connections with people.
